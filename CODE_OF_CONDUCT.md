# Ranu.js Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the **Ranu.js community** a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
* Learning from experience and improving our behavior
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall Ranu.js community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing another person's private information without their explicit permission
* Deliberate intimidation, threats, or sustained disruption of community discussions
* Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community

## Community Expectations

Ranu.js is an open-source technical project.

Technical disagreement, architectural debate, code review, and constructive criticism are normal and valuable parts of open-source development.

We expect participants to:

* Discuss ideas rather than attack individuals
* Keep technical discussions constructive and relevant
* Respect maintainers, contributors, users, and other community participants
* Give constructive feedback in good faith
* Accept reasonable moderation decisions
* Avoid repeatedly disrupting productive technical discussions

Disagreement is welcome. Harassment and personal attacks are not.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior they determine to be inappropriate, threatening, offensive, harmful, or inconsistent with this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within Ranu.js community spaces and also applies when an individual is officially representing the Ranu.js community in public spaces.

Community spaces may include:

* GitHub Issues
* Pull requests
* GitHub Discussions
* Project documentation
* Official community channels
* Other collaboration spaces operated by the Ranu.js project

Examples of officially representing the project may include using an official project account, speaking on behalf of Ranu.js, or acting as an appointed representative of the project.

## Reporting Conduct Issues

Instances of abusive, harassing, threatening, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the Ranu.js project maintainers.

Please avoid posting sensitive Code of Conduct complaints publicly through GitHub Issues or Discussions.

Reports should include, where possible:

* A description of what happened
* Where the incident occurred
* Relevant links or references
* Any additional context that may help the maintainers understand the situation

A dedicated community conduct contact channel may be published as the Ranu.js community grows.

Until then, reports should be made through an available private contact channel for the project maintainers.

> Security vulnerabilities are handled separately. Please follow [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) for security-related reports.

## Enforcement

All Code of Conduct reports will be reviewed by the project maintainers.

Maintainers will seek to handle reports fairly and appropriately based on the nature, context, severity, and frequency of the reported behavior.

Possible actions may include:

* A private clarification or warning
* Removal or moderation of inappropriate content
* Temporary restrictions on community participation
* Rejection of contributions
* Temporary or permanent removal from project-controlled community spaces

The appropriate response will depend on the circumstances of each case.

## Privacy

Maintainers will make reasonable efforts to respect the privacy of people who report Code of Conduct concerns.

Information may be shared with other maintainers when necessary to investigate or address a report.

Reporters should avoid including unnecessary personal or sensitive information.
